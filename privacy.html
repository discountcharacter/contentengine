Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

ContentEngine is a private tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channels. This page explains what it can access when a Google account is connected to it, what it does with that access, and how to remove it.

What ContentEngine asks for

When you connect a Google account, ContentEngine asks for two YouTube permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload), to upload videos to the channel.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl), to set the title, description, tags and release time of the videos it uploads, add them to playlists on the same channel, and read the view counts of the channel's videos.

It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

What it does with that access

  • Uploads videos that the channel owner made, and edits the details of those videos.
  • Adds those videos to playlists on the same channel.
  • Reads view, like and comment counts for the channel's videos and saves them in a report on the owner's computer.

It does not read comments, messages or subscriber lists, and it does not like, comment, subscribe or post anywhere on your behalf beyond the uploads described above.

Where the data is kept

ContentEngine runs only on its owner's computer. The access token Google returns is saved in a local file on that computer, and the view counts are saved in local report files. Nothing is sent to any server run by ContentEngine, because there is none.

Data received from Google is never sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

ContentEngine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

ContentEngine uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove ContentEngine's access at any time in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections (also reachable through security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Once access is removed, the saved token stops working. The owner deletes the local token file and reports when a channel is disconnected, and you can ask for deletion at any time using the contact below.
